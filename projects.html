EnStyle Tool

Nstyle will serve as an online extension, designed to enhance
the shopping experience across various online clothing platforms. Upon selecting
a clothing item on a particular brand's site, users will be presented with a
pop-up that displays their recommended size based on the measurements they
provided during the initial setup. These measures are stored in a database
and need only be entered once, though they can be updated as necessary. Each time
the user engages with a partnered shopping platform, the pop-up will automatically
appear, offering tailored sizing recommendations specific to the selected brand.
The website for this project has been completed. However, it continues to be an
ongoing project. The inspiration behind EnStyle came from the common experience
of online shoppers buying clothes in their size only to realize the brand's
measurements did not match their expectations.

Budgeting App

This budgeting app serves as a financial assistant for those that struggle
to understand how to budget or how to do certain financial calculations.
The app comes equipped with features such as calculating a budget according
to the famous 50/30/20 rule, calculating the future value of an account,
calculating life insurance needs, and a quick and easy game that tests and
educates users on financial concepts. The inspiration for this budgeting app
came from the large percentage of individuals who are financially illiterate
due to lack of access to resources. Simple apps like this can be used to
educate individuals over the importance of financial literacy. (Please be advised that this app is not 100% accurate)
