InStyle

Once finalized, Nstyle will serve as an online
extension, designed to enhance the shopping experience
across various online clothing platforms.
Upon selecting a clothing item on a particular
brand's site, users will be presented with a pop-up
that displays their recommended size based on
the measurements they provided during the initial
setup. These measures are stored in a database and
need only be entered once, though they can be
updated as necessary. Each time the user engages
with a partnered shopping platform, the pop-up
will automatically appear, offering tailored sizing
recommendations specific to the selected brand.
**This project is still ongoing.
